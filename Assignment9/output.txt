**THE SMARTEST DOG**

TOM THOUGHT HIS DOG, MAX, WAS THE SMARTEST DOG IN THE WORLD.

ONE DAY, TOM SAID, “MAX, GO TO THE STORE AND BUY SOME MILK.”

MAX BARKED CONFIDENTLY AND RAN OUT THE DOOR.

TEN MINUTES LATER, MAX CAME BACK… WITH A PIZZA.

TOM WAS SHOCKED. “MAX! I SAID MILK, NOT PIZZA!”

MAX TILTED HIS HEAD, LOOKING CONFUSED.

TOM SIGHED. “FINE… AT LEAST YOU TRIED.”

THE NEXT DAY, TOM TRIED AGAIN. “MAX, GO GET MILK!”

MAX RAN OUT EVEN FASTER THIS TIME.

FIVE MINUTES LATER, MAX RETURNED… WITH TWO PIZZAS.

TOM YELLED, “WHY TWO PIZZAS?!”

MAX BARKED PROUDLY.

TOM LOOKED CLOSER… AND NOTICED SOMETHING.

THERE WAS A NOTE ATTACHED TO THE BOX:

> “YOUR DOG IS AMAZING. HE PAID WITH YOUR CREDIT CARD.”
